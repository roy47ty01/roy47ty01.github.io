Privacy policy

watch-sync — effective 16 November 2026

This policy covers a single-user personal application (“the app”) operated by one individual to read their own health data from the Google Health API. It has exactly one user: the person who operates it. No other person's data is collected, processed or accessible.

1. What data is accessed

The app reads, and only reads, the operator's own Google Health data:

  • Activity and fitness data, including step counts and exercise sessions
  • Sleep sessions and sleep stages
  • Heart rate measurements

Access is granted by the operator through Google's own OAuth consent screen, using these read-only permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/googlehealth.sleep.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly

The app cannot write to, modify or delete anything in Google Health.

2. Why it is accessed

Solely so the operator can keep a long-term personal record of their own sleep, activity and heart rate, and be notified when a scheduled sync completes. There is no other purpose.

3. Where it is stored

Retrieved data is written to a private repository on GitHub that only the operator can access, plus private records kept by the app's authentication provider (Google) and, if configured, a notification message containing no health data beyond a completion status.

Authentication credentials are held as encrypted secrets in that private repository. They are never written to a public location, a web page, or a log.

4. What is never done with it

  • It is not sold, rented or shared with any third party.
  • It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models.
  • It is not published, and no part of it is visible to the public.
  • It is not transferred to any other service or country beyond Google's own storage.

This website itself sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and contains no tracking of any kind.

5. How long it is kept

Indefinitely, until the operator deletes it. Individual records can be removed at any time by deleting them from the private repository; the app does not maintain separate archives.

6. Your control over the data

The operator can revoke the app's access to Google Health at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all future retrieval immediately. The operator may also delete the private repository, which removes the stored copy.

Because the app's only user is the account owner, there are no other data subjects and no third-party requests to fulfil.

7. Security

Access tokens and client credentials are stored as encrypted secrets, are not embedded in any client-side code, and are not transmitted anywhere except to Google's API endpoints over HTTPS. The data store is a private repository with no public read access.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and has no users other than its operator.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Since the app has a single user, notice is inherently direct.
